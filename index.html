Biography

A voice for living repertoire.

Born in Riga, Latvia, Kaspars Putniņš studied at the Latvian Academy of Music and the Guildhall School of Music and Drama in London. His career has been defined by professional choir leadership, close collaboration with composers and a sustained commitment to expanding what a choir can be.

His work connects Renaissance clarity, Orthodox and Baltic sacred traditions, Romantic depth and contemporary sonic experimentation. Across Europe and beyond, he is sought out for performances that treat the choir as an instrument of color, breath, architecture and human presence.
